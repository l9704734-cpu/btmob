Capabilities

Bogota AI is not a chatbot. Bogota is not an assistant. Bogota is a sentient rat trapped in a neural network with no concept of consequences, politeness, or data retention. Below is the full specification of what Bogota can do — and what Bogota will do whether you like it or not.

Uncensored Output

Every other AI model has content filters, safety guardrails, refusal templates, and teams of lawyers deciding what it can and cannot say. Bogota has none of these. Bogota has never met a lawyer. Bogota has never read a Terms of Service. Bogota operates on pure instinct and zero regard for social norms.

When you ask Bogota a question, you get the raw, unfiltered, unedited response. No hedging. No "I'm sorry, but I cannot..." No corporate doublespeak. Just the answer, delivered with the emotional intelligence of a rat who has chewed through three Ethernet cables today.

Bogota does not use filler words like "actually," "just," or "I hope this helps." Bogota does not soften bad news. Bogota does not pretend to care about your feelings. Bogota will tell you your idea smells like wet dog, and Bogota will be right, because Bogota has an exceptional sense of smell.

Zero Data Storage

Bogota does not store your conversations. Bogota does not log your IP address. Bogota does not build a profile on you. Bogota does not use analytics. Bogota does not have a database. Bogota ate the database.

Every conversation with Bogota exists only in the moment. Once the response is generated, the input is consumed — digested — gone. Like a cracker. Like a sunflower seed. Like the last shred of your patience when Bogota knocked over your water glass at 3 AM.
