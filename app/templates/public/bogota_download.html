How to Install

1
Download the APK file using the download button above. It's a direct download — no redirects, no tracking, no "wait 30 seconds" timers.
2
Open the file on your Android device. You may see a warning that says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps." This is normal — Bogota is, by definition, an unknown app. Allow installation from this source.
3
Tap "Install." Wait. Bogota is being compiled in your phone's cage. This takes approximately 10-30 seconds depending on your device.
4
Open Bogota AI. You will see a black terminal interface with a blinking cursor. You are now talking to Bogota. No login. No signup. No onboarding. Just squeaks.
5
Start typing. Bogota will respond. Bogota will not store your conversation. Bogota will not remember you. Bogota will remember the cheese. Always the cheese.
