Frequently Asked Questions

Is the free version actually free or is there a catch?
There is no catch. Bogota AI is free. Uncensored. Offline. No ads. No data selling. No "upgrade to unlock features." The core experience — uncensored chat, offline inference, zero data storage, no tracking — is and always will be free. The paid tiers exist for developers who need API access, extended context windows, or self-hosted deployment.
Do the paid tiers remove any restrictions on what Bogota will answer?
No. Bogota AI is uncensored on every tier — free, Starter, Pro, and Enterprise. There is no "premium uncensored" mode because there is no "censored" mode. The paid tiers add technical features (API access, larger context windows, self-hosting) — not content access. Bogota answers everything on every plan.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free gives you unlimited uncensored chat, offline inference, and 32K token context — all on-device with no data stored. Pro adds unlimited API requests, 128K token context window, session-based conversation memory, and custom system prompts. If you're using Bogota as a chat app, Free is enough. If you're building with the API, Pro is for you.
Why would I pay for Enterprise?
Enterprise lets you self-host Bogota AI on your own infrastructure. No external API calls. No third-party dependencies. Full control over the model, the data, and the deployment. Plus custom fine-tuning, unlimited context, SLA guarantees, and direct developer support. For organizations that need on-premise AI with zero external data flow, Enterprise is the only option.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime. No contracts. No cancellation fees. No lock-in. If you cancel, you revert to the Free tier — which is still uncensored, still private, still offline. You never lose the core Bogota AI experience.
Do you store my payment information?
No. Payment processing is handled by a third-party provider (Stripe). Bogota AI does not see or store your card number, billing address, or banking details. The payment provider handles the transaction — Bogota AI only receives a webhook confirming the payment succeeded.
Is there a student or nonprofit discount?
Yes. Students with a .edu email get Pro for free. Registered nonprofits get 50% off Enterprise. Contact support with your credentials and we'll set it up within 24 hours.
