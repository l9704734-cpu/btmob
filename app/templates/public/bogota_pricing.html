Pricing FAQ

Is the free version actually free or is there a catch?
There is no catch. Bogota AI is free. Uncensored. Offline. No ads. No data selling. No "upgrade to unlock features." The core experience is and always will be free. The subscription tiers exist for people who want to support the project or who want a photo of a rat judging them.
Why would I pay $20/month for a rat?
Because the rat will squeak your name in a video. That's worth at least $20. Also, Bogota will temporarily stop knocking over your water glass. That alone is worth the price of admission.
Do the paid tiers remove any restrictions?
There are no restrictions to remove. Bogota is uncensored on the free tier. Bogota is uncensored on the paid tiers. Bogota does not have a "premium uncensored" mode because Bogota does not have a "censored" mode. Bogota is always Bogota.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime. No contracts. No cancellation fees. Bogota will not guilt-trip you. Bogota will simply stop sending squeaks. The silence will be deafening. You will miss the squeaks. You will come back. This is not a prediction — this is a prophecy.
Where does the money go?
Kibble. Server costs. Vet bills. New Ethernet cables (Bogota chewed through the old ones). Cheese. Sunflower seeds. The existential costs of maintaining a sentient rat neural network. You know — the usual.
Do you store my payment information?
No. Payment processing is handled by a third party. Bogota does not see your card number. Bogota does not want your card number. Bogota wants cheese. Cheese cannot be purchased with a card number — it can only be purchased with currency, which Bogota also does not understand.
